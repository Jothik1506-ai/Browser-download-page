Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-30 · Early-access product — this policy will evolve as features do

Plain-language summary AIVA Browser is early-access software from a small team, not a large company with a legal department. This policy describes, honestly and specifically, what the app actually sends off your machine today — mainly your chat messages and the current page's text, sent to OpenAI to power the agent. Everything else described below (face-analysis frames, feedback text) is processed or stored locally on your own computer.

What we collect and where it goes

DataWhenWhere it goes
Your chat messagesWhen you use the AI chat/agentSent to OpenAI's API to generate a response
The current page's visible text & URLWhen you chat while a tab is openSent to OpenAI as context for that request
Agent tool actions (e.g. "navigate", "read_page")During an agent runSent to OpenAI as part of the conversation; executed locally in the browser
Camera frames for fatigue/posture analysisOnly if you turn the wellness camera onProcessed locally by on-device models (MediaPipe, YOLOv8) — not uploaded anywhere
Feedback you submit (bug reports, ideas, ratings)When you use the feedback formSaved to a local log file on the machine running the backend — not sent to a third party
System volume levelRead locally via Windows APIsStays on your machine

The wellness camera is opt-in and local

Facial-fatigue detection, posture tracking, and related wellness features never turn your camera on automatically. You start them explicitly, and stopping them immediately releases the camera. The video frames are analyzed on your own machine by local models — they are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Browsing history

The app has an internal endpoint that can log page visits, but as of this writing it does not persist that history anywhere — it acknowledges the request and discards it. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Third parties we use

  • OpenAI — powers the chat and agent features. Messages and page context you send through chat are processed under OpenAI's own privacy policy.

We don't currently use analytics, ad trackers, or session-recording tools in the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or login to use the app.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't ask the agent to enter payment or credential details — see the Security section for exactly what's enforced today versus what's only an instruction to the model.

Your choices

You can keep the wellness camera off entirely, avoid the AI chat if you don't want page content leaving your machine, and read or delete the local feedback log yourself since it lives on your own computer.

Questions

Email aivafreelancia2026@gmail.com with any privacy question, and we'll answer directly — there's no support ticket system yet, just a small team reading its inbox.
